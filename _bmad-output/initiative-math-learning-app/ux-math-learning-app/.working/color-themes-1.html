Pick a color theme

Five purple-based themes for the math journey app, each with a different feel. Every card shows the same screen: today's journey stop, a message from her companion, a drag-the-tiles equation, the buttons, a correct-answer message and the day's progress bar. Which one feels most like hers? She can also mix them, for example "the background of 2 with the purple of 4."
