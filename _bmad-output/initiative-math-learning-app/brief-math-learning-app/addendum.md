## Common Core Grade 8 content map

The CCSS Grade 8 math standards cover five domains and 28 standards. The Common Core treats three areas as the critical focus for the year: (1) linear equations and systems, (2) functions, and (3) geometry, including the Pythagorean theorem and volume.

| Domain | Standards | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **The Number System (8.NS)** | 8.NS.1–2 | Rational vs. irrational numbers; converting repeating decimals to fractions; estimating irrationals such as √2 and π on a number line |
| **Expressions & Equations (8.EE)** | 8.EE.1–8 | Integer exponent rules; square and cube roots; scientific notation; proportional relationships and slope; deriving y = mx + b; solving one-variable linear equations (one, none, or infinitely many solutions); systems of two linear equations (graphing, substitution, elimination) |
| **Functions (8.F)** | 8.F.1–5 | What a function is; comparing functions in different representations; linear vs. nonlinear; building functions from a context (rate of change, initial value); describing graphs qualitatively |
| **Geometry (8.G)** | 8.G.1–9 | Rotations, reflections, and translations; congruence and similarity through transformations; angle facts (triangle angle sum, exterior angles, parallel lines cut by a transversal); the Pythagorean theorem and its converse, including distance on the coordinate plane; volume of cones, cylinders, and spheres |
| **Statistics & Probability (8.SP)** | 8.SP.1–4 | Scatter plots, association, and outliers; informally fitting a line of best fit; using linear models; two-way tables |

Source: Common Core State Standards for Mathematics, Grade 8 (corestandards.org). This map was written from memory of the published standard and has not been checked line by line against the source. Check it before writing content.

## Prerequisite gaps worth checking (grades 6–7)

Students who find 8th-grade math hard are often missing earlier skills rather than 8th-grade ones:

- Operations with negative numbers and fractions (7.NS)
- Ratios, rates, and proportional reasoning (6.RP, 7.RP)
- Writing and simplifying expressions, and solving one- and two-step equations (6.EE, 7.EE)
